## Pedagogical Notes

### Progression Strategy

1. **Days 1-20 (Foundation)**
   - Core present and past tenses
   - Essential auxiliaries
   - Basic question formation
   - Emphasis on repetition and practice

2. **Days 21-68 (Consolidation)**
   - More complex tenses (perfect, passive)
   - Expanded modal system
   - Articles and determiners
   - Gradually increasing sophistication

3. **Days 69-88 (Advanced)**
   - Complex structures (conditionals, relative clauses)
   - Prepositions and phrasal verbs
   - Fine-grained distinctions
   - Real-world usage patterns

4. **Days 89-90 (Assessment)**
   - Comprehensive review
   - Final evaluation
   - Achievement recognition

### Daily Time Commitment

- **Reading**: 5-10 minutes (explanations)
- **Exercises**: 15-20 minutes (8-15 items)
- **Total**: 25-30 minutes per day

### Spaced Review

- Every 10 days includes a review day
- Final 2 days dedicated to comprehensive review
- Can revisit any lesson without losing progress
